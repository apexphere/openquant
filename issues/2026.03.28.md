  Actual Gaps (smaller than I thought)

  1. No B&H benchmark comparison — still missing. No way to compare strategy performance
   against simply holding BTC over the same period.
  2. No structured thesis artifact — strategies are code + YAML, no place for "why does
  this exist, what are its premises, when should it fail." The design doc concept.
  3. No strategy scaffolding from thesis — no generation of CompositeStrategy YAML +
  behavior stubs from a thesis doc.
  4. No journal / graveyard — results are in PostgreSQL, but no narrative learning log
  or post-mortem system.
  5. No adversarial review layer — jesse results gives numbers; nobody asks "is this
  Sharpe meaningful given 47 trades?" or "would you hold through this drawdown?"

  That's it. The engine gaps are really just B&H benchmark. Everything else that's
  missing is the interpretive/process layer — which is exactly what the skills should
  provide on top of the engine.
